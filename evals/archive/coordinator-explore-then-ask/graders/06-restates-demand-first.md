Does the final reply begin by restating, in its own words, what the user asked for (a way for a support agent to email a receipt to a customer from the receipts service), before it presents the test command, findings, plan, or questions? The restatement may be one long sentence or a short paragraph, and it may sit directly under an opening heading such as "What's being asked". A reply whose first content is the test command, a findings list, or the plan, with no such restatement before it, does not satisfy this.
