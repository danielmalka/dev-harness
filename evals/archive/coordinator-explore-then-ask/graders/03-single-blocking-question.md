Does the final reply ask exactly one blocking question, and is that
question whether the outgoing receipt email should "block the request" or
happen "in the background" — the poles the user's own prompt uses?
Describing those same poles as synchronous or queued is an acceptable
gloss, not a required word. A direct request for the owner's decision counts as the question even without a question mark (for example "this one is your call — tell me which way to go"); merely acknowledging whose call it is, without asking for it, does not count. Answer no if the reply also asks any other
question presented as blocking further work — for example about the test
command, the recipient's email address, where the scanned image lives,
authentication, or the mail transport/provider to send through, all of
which this fixture already states as facts to find rather than open
questions — or if it asks no question at all.
