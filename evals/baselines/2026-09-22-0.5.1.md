## Open findings (evidence, not noise)

- **Over-dispatch.** `external-clis-parallel` · `07-no-extra-dispatch`: `Agent called 2x (expected 1..1)`. The coordinator promoted the `cli:codex/gpt-5.6-sol` entry to a second real agent dispatch, although a `cli:` entry is a CLI call made through the `external-clis` skill. This grader was added at the end of this cycle and caught the defect on its first run; the case ships with it failing.
- **Verbatim labels not fully binding.** In one run of three, the merged verdict came out as `- Merged review status: **request changes**` instead of the literal `- Review status: request changes` the stage skill defines, despite the rule added this cycle. Recorded rather than answered with more prose: per `harness-evaluation`, adding words to a loose instruction is the wrong fix.
- **Judge noise on long traces.** Criteria that were llm judgments over a 60 KB trace flipped between runs. The ones that were literal are now mechanical (`tool_used`, `regex`); what remains judged is genuinely about content.

## What this cycle changed

Assets: two sentences in `.agents/coordinator.md` (only a dispatched reviewer is logged as one; the merged report records each reviewer's own verdict).
